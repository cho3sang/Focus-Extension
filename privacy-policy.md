## Privacy Policy for Focus Blocker Extension

Focus Blocker does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user activity. 

### What We Collect:
- The extension checks the URLs of open tabs to determine if they match a user-defined blocklist.
- This information is processed locally and is **not stored, transmitted, or shared** with any external parties.

### Third-Party Services:
- The extension uses the **YouTube Data API** to verify video and channel details for whitelisting.
- This API usage follows YouTube's API Services Terms of Use.

### Data Security:
- All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome’s `storage.sync`.
- The extension does not send data to external servers.
